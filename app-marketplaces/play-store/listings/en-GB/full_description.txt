Your money, under control. Expenses, budgets, investments, debts, and net worth. One app. All on your phone.

Monekin is private, ad-free, and open source. No sign-up. No cloud. No bank. Your data never leaves your device.

Here's what you can do:

<b>The basics</b>

• <b>No limits:</b> unlimited transactions and accounts.
• <b>Recurring:</b> stay on top of bills and subscriptions.
• <b>Transfers:</b> move money between accounts, balances stay right.
• <b>Your way:</b> categories, subcategories, and hundreds of icons.
• <b>Multi-currency:</b> 50+ currencies with your own exchange rates.

<b>Your whole net worth</b>

• <b>Net worth:</b> cash, properties, investments, and debts in one place.
• <b>Real investing:</b> stocks, funds, and crypto, trade by trade or from a simple snapshot.
• <b>Debts & goals:</b> keep growth and obligations in view.
• <b>Budgets:</b> set limits and see where the money goes.

<b>Understand your money</b>

• <b>Charts:</b> stats for the accounts and periods you care about.
• <b>Financial Health:</b> looks at savings, debt, cash flow, and investing, not just whether you spent less than you earned.

<b>Yours, and yours alone</b>

• <b>No ads, no tracking:</b> and no mandatory account.
• <b>No bank sync, by design:</b> we never connect to your bank.
• <b>Local backups:</b> unlimited, kept by you.

Monekin helps you save and stay in control, without turning your finances into someone else's product.
